The house

Room for the whole boat crew.

About 1,500 square feet, laid out for long days on the water. The open kitchen and living room keep everyone together, with a dining table for six and an island that seats three more for coffee and tide-chart planning.

Master bedroom

Queen bed, a television, and a private bathroom with a five-foot walk-in shower. Room to rinse off the river.

Second bedroom

Another queen bed for the second couple, or the friend who always gets the first fish.

Bunk room

Bunk beds in the utility room off the master. Kids love it, and so does anyone with an early tide to catch.

Kitchen & living

Open plan, with a table for six and an island for three. Cook the crab you caught that afternoon.
